About Me

My earliest memories are of the room with the jackalope on the wall. Within that chamber of carpeted walls and tiled ceilings there was, on a little two-legged table, a tube. It was a tube of tongue-paste. Being left with no sustenance beyond this viscous and garlicky concoction, I did what any child of more or less my years may or may not have done: I lathered it over the glass floor and drew cariacatures of platypods in it with the reverse side of my toenail.
